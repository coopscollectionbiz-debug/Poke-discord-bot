Pokémon tier review: Gens 1–9

Rule: a tier measures collectible prestige (scarcity, role in the games, popularity). Base stat total (BST) is the whole of a Pokémon's battle strength (ordinary Pokémon have no abilities in battle; ranked battles run at Lv 50), so BST acts as a guardrail: it moves a species only when its stats are far outside its tier. Mythic = an official Mythical, or a cover legendary with BST ≥ 660. Every species, from today's tier (live; only Gens 1–5 can drop) to the proposed tier on branch audit/pokemon-tiers. A gold border means at least one player owns it (Nov 9 snapshot, seq 604). Nobody loses a Pokémon; owners see the new tier label, and future donations, evolutions and drop odds use the new tier.
