Dashboard review: Teams, Collection, Overview, movesets, ranked

Everything below is the real dashboard on a local bot with a fake player (no production data), branch feature/teams-battle-team (PR #67, draft). Click any image for full size. What to look at in each section is listed above its images.

Overview (new home page)

  • Where /dashboard now lands. Ranked card (rating, W/L, win rate, streak, leaderboard position), casual record, rewards waiting, both teams (Battle team at Lv 50), Pokédex completion per live region.
  • The fake player hasn't played a ranked battle yet, so it reads 1,000 and “Not ranked yet”.

Teams: Display and Battle teams (design option B)

  • Switch between the two teams at the top; the box on the right adds to the team on screen (tap, or drag onto a slot). Drag between slots to reorder, or onto the box to remove.
  • Battle team: Lv 50 with the real Level small, rule chips (one mythic, one Mega), mythic tiles locked in the box with a corner badge, type chips everywhere.
  • New on every slot: Moves (edits that team's moveset) and, while Mega Evolution is on, the Mega picker (here “Mega: Mewtwo Y”). Each team keeps its own Mega and moves.

Movesets (per team, copy from the other team)

  • Up to 4 distinct moves the Pokémon has learned by its real Level (TMs will be added to this list later). Shows type, power and the Level each was learned; the rest disable at 4.
  • “Copy from Battle team / Display team” appears when the other team has a moveset; “Use automatic moves” goes back to today's behaviour.
  • Casual battles use the Display team's moves, ranked and wager battles the Battle team's. This also fixes ranked fighters knowing their Lv 50 moves.

Collection (replaces the Pokémon page, design option A)

  • A real Pokédex: dex numbers, unowned shown as silhouettes, D#/B# team badges, region chips with completion, Owned / Missing / Shiny only. Evolve, Donate, Convert and bulk donate are unchanged.
  • Browse mode: tap a Pokémon for its details (drawer on desktop, sheet on phone): copies and team slots, normal/shiny toggle, stats at Level and Lv 50 Power, both teams' moves, evolution chain, and Evolve / Donate / Convert / Use on a team.
  • Teams are no longer edited here (no Change Team mode).

Achievements: Ranked and Casual shown separately

  • Tabs: All / Ranked / Casual / Collection & more, with a gold count when rewards are ready.
  • Ranked = the existing battle achievements (now “ranked or wager” battles; progress keeps every season's wins across the rating reset). Casual = 8 new ones: Friendly Match, Sparring Partner I–IV, Warm Streak, Crowd Favourite, Untouchable. No rewards on the casual ones yet: they're added after your approval, like the others.

Discord: /showbattlecard, and the backdrops on the dashboard

  • /showbattlecard (option 2): Battle team at Lv 50 with real Levels, rating, record, position, streak and Power; the empty card for players without a Battle team.
  • Trainers page: owned Epic+ trainers get the same tier backdrop as the Discord trainer card (shiny Pokémon get the Discord motes and stars on every page above).

Not shown as stills: drag and drop (mouse) and the animations. Open the dashboard on the local bot to try them.
