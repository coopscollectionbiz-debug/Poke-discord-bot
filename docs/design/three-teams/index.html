Three Teams and Card Cleanup

The fighting team splits into a Showcase team (favorites, never fights), a Casual team (old Display team) and a Ranked team (old Battle team). This page has the decisions so far, mockups for every card that changes, and the full list of display commands with what happens to each.

The mockups are HTML drawings that follow the card tokens in utils/render/tokens.js, not real renders. Screenshots marked “today” are real previews from docs/design/previews.

Team pick · Casual or Ranked

Trainer encounters and /challenge

  • Encounter: the card shows both prizes. One click picks the team and starts the fight, and the Trainer's Levels come from the team picked. Class, name, party size and lines are rolled at spawn as today.
  • /challenge: the private preview shows your Casual team; the buttons pick the team. The pick holds for a whole Elite Four run.
  • Both teams fight at real Levels here. A player with only one team set sees only that button and prize.

Merge · /inspectpokemon → /pokedex

/pokedex with search that forgives typos

  • Autocomplete while typing: up to 25 matches by name prefix, then by any part of the name, plus dex numbers. Pokémon you own come first.
  • Typo match when a misspelled name is sent anyway: a clear winner opens directly, otherwise three “Did you mean” buttons.
  • One card, three buttons: Entry (today's /pokedex), Your stats (today's /inspectpokemon), Shiny. /inspectpokemon stays as an alias opening on Your stats for about two weeks.

Rework · /showtrainers retired

  • Browsing your whole collection moves to the dashboard Trainers page.

Copy Showcase into Ranked team?

Ranked teams allow each Pokémon once, one Mega and one mythic-tier Pokémon. These won't be copied:
  • Caterpie (slot 5): already on the team
  • Mega Gengar and Mega Lucario come over as Gengar and Lucario (Mega Charizard X keeps the one Mega)
