# Gen 7 (Alola) release — encounter probability before/after

Generated by `npm run report:balance -- --gen7` from `pokemonData.json` and the live weights/multipliers in `utils/weightedRandom.js`. **Exact** values (no sampling); `tests/gen69Balance.test.js` checks the formula against the real selector.

How the selector works: every species gets its tier's weight (× rank and ball multipliers). A tier's share of draws is therefore *(species in the tier × weight) ÷ total weight* — adding 88 species raises the share of tiers they join and lowers everything else, and splits each tier among more species. Quests pick uniformly; the weekly pack and starter pack use fixed tier slots.

## Reading the numbers

- Tier shares move modestly because Alola's 88 species skew rare/epic/legendary: e.g. Poké Ball, no rank, Legendary 0.46% → 0.54%, Common 55.38% → 55.39%.
- Each existing species becomes rarer: 91.7% of today's odds for every species under the weighted selector (the total weight grows uniformly for all tiers), 88.1% under quests, and per tier for pack slots (common 91.7%, uncommon 93.8%, rare 87.7%).
- No proposed balance change is applied; these are the consequences of the existing rules.
