## Update: Fairy type arrives ✨

**New: the Fairy type.**
- Fairy is now the 18th type. It's strong against Dragon, Dark and Fighting, and Dragon moves can't touch it.
- 22 Pokémon you already know now have their modern typing: Clefairy, Clefable, Cleffa, Jigglypuff, Wigglytuff, Igglybuff, Togepi, Togetic, Togekiss, Marill, Azumarill, Azurill, Snubbull, Granbull, Ralts, Kirlia, Gardevoir, Mr. Mime, Mime Jr., Mawile, Cottonee and Whimsicott.
- Most of them can now learn Fairy moves like Moonblast, Dazzling Gleam and Play Rough as they level up.

**Battles.** Type matchups now follow the modern chart. Steel no longer resists Ghost or Dark. Everything else about your Pokémon's stats and moves stays the same.

**Your collection is safe.** No Pokémon, Level, shiny or achievement was removed or reset.

**Achievements.**
- New: **Fairy Specialist** (own 15 Fairy-type Pokémon) and **Type Encyclopedia II** (15 of every type, Fairy included).
- If you already qualify, they're recognised automatically.
- Anything you earned before stays earned, even where a Pokémon changed type.

**Also:**
- `/pokedex` now ignores accents and punctuation, so `flabebe` and `type null` both work.
- Sprite credits are at `/credits`.
