# Patch Notes: Held Items 🧿

**Your Pokémon can now hold items!** Give each Pokémon on your teams an item that helps it in battle, all from the Dashboard's **Teams** page.

## Eight free items for everyone

Open **Dashboard → Teams** and you'll unlock eight held items right away, for free:

| Item | What it does |
|---|---|
| 🍖 **Leftovers** | Restores 1/16 of max HP at the end of every turn. |
| 🍋 **Sitrus Berry** | Restores 1/4 of max HP when HP falls to half or less (once per battle). |
| 🎗️ **Focus Sash** | At full HP, survives a knockout hit with 1 HP (once per battle). |
| 🦅 **Quick Claw** | 20% chance each turn to move first among moves of the same priority. |
| 🥋 **Expert Belt** | Super-effective attacks deal 1.2× damage. |
| ✨ **Bright Powder** | Moves aimed at the holder are 10% less accurate. |
| 🎀 **Focus Band** | 10% chance to survive a knockout hit with 1 HP. |
| 🥚 **Lucky Egg** | The holder earns 1.5× battle EXP. |

## Items are permanent unlocks

- **Unlock an item once and use it on every team.** Your Leftovers can be on Snorlax in your Display team *and* on Blissey in your Battle team.
- **One per team:** only one Pokémon on a team can hold the same item. If you pick an item a team-mate is holding, you'll be offered to **move** it.
- **Nothing is ever used up.** Berries and Focus Sash work once per battle and are ready again next battle.
- A Pokémon set to **Mega Evolve** uses its Mega Stone in the item slot, so it can't hold a regular item on that team.

## How to equip

1. Open **Dashboard → Teams** and pick your Display or Battle team.
2. Tap **＋ Add item** (or the round badge) on a Pokémon.
3. Search or filter (Offense, Defense, Recovery, Speed & utility, Type, Species), then tap **Equip**.

Changes save right away. Your team doesn't need to be saved again. You can only manage items on the dashboard (there are no item commands in Discord), but your battle replays show items when they activate.

## More items in the Shop

Visit **Dashboard → Shop → Held items** to unlock 25 more. Each is bought once and works on every team:

- **18 type items** (Charcoal, Mystic Water, Magnet, Spell Tag…): 1.2× power for moves of their type.
- **Muscle Band / Wise Glasses:** 1.1× power for physical / special moves.
- **Eviolite:** 1.5× Defense and Sp. Def for Pokémon that can still evolve.
- **Shell Bell:** heals 1/8 of the damage it deals.
- **Black Sludge:** heals Poison types every turn (but hurts everyone else!).
- **Light Ball** (Pikachu) and **Thick Club** (Cubone and Marowak): double their attack.

## Fine print

- Items don't change your team's Power rating or the underdog surge.
- Ranked battles still put everyone at Lv 50.
- Lucky Egg only boosts battle EXP. It doesn't change CC, TP, achievements or catches.
