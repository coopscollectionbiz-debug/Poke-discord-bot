# Battle FX Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Discord battle replays of full-sim fights animate status conditions and stat changes as beats with their own text lines, and keep status tags and stat chips on the field.

**Architecture:** The engine records each turn's conditions into `result.conditions`. A pure `turnTimeline()` turns one turn's steps into timed segments (hits, beats, lines), and playback uses it both to pace the next update and as the render thread's input. `renderBattleFrame` walks the segments, and `utils/battle/fx.js` draws each effect.

**Tech Stack:** Node ESM, `@pkmn/sim` 0.10.12 (pinned), `@napi-rs/canvas`, the repo's own GIF encoder (`utils/render/gif.js`), and the repo's test harness (`tests/harness.js`, `npm run test:safe`).

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-13-battle-fx-design.md`

## Global Constraints

- `BATTLE_FX` is on by default. `BATTLE_FX=0` gives exactly today's turn images and pacing.
- Full-sim results (`v: 2` with `steps`) only. Today's engine and the Mega effects engine render exactly as before.
- No new dependency, and no new patch on `@pkmn/sim`.
- Beat 8 frames. Floor 4 frames. Read margin 600 ms. GIF margin `ANIMATION.turnMs − PLAYBACK.delayMs` (2,000 ms). No pacing change for an image without beats.
- Player-facing wording comes from `utils/battle/narrate.js`, in the games' and Showdown's wording. Copy uses straight quotes, no em dashes, and American English.
- Glossary terms: **Status condition** and **Stat change**. Never "stage" or "stat boost" in prose. `boosts` stays only as Showdown's own field name.
- Commits carry the user's name only, with no Claude trailer.

## Review Focus

1. A status that lands on a switch-in (Toxic Spikes): the beat must play on the Pokémon that just came in, with the spotlight already on it. Test in Task 3.
2. A fight that ends on a residual knockout (a burn KO mid-turn): the last image's KO beat grays the sprite, and the result image shows no tag for it. Tests in Tasks 3 and 5.
3. Called moves (Metronome, Sleep Talk) and multi-hit moves: the n-th move step must stay the n-th log event. Test in Task 3, an invariant over seeded fights.
4. Stat chips on a Mega slot: the chips must not draw over the MEGA tag. Test in Task 5.
5. The timeline crossing to a render thread: it must be plain data. Test in Task 3 (`structuredClone` round-trip).

### Task 3: The timeline

**Files:**
- Create: `utils/battle/timeline.js`
- Test: `tests/battleTimeline.test.js` (new)

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `result.conditions` (Task 1), `statChangesText` (Task 2), `stepText` (`narrate.js`), and `ANIMATION` (`render.js`). Move `ANIMATION` to `timeline.js` and re-export it from `render.js`, so `timeline.js` never imports the canvas. `PLAYBACK` comes from `tuning.js`.
- Produces, all in `timeline.js`:
  - `battleFx(env = process.env) -> boolean`: `env.BATTLE_FX !== "0"`.
  - `FX = Object.freeze({ beatFrames: 8, minBeatFrames: 4, readMs: 600, capFrames: Infinity })`. `capFrames` is set from the bench in Task 7.
  - `Marks = { status: string[2][], chips: (Record<string, number> | null)[2] }`.
  - `marksAt(result, index) -> Marks | null`: from `conditions[index]` (null without `conditions`), with chips taken from `active[s].boosts`.
  - `hitsTimeline(events, hpBefore, active) -> Timeline`: today's image, hits only, with no marks.
  - `turnTimeline(result, turn, names, { env, capFrames = FX.capFrames } = {}) -> Timeline`.
  - `Timeline = { segments: Segment[], end: { hp, marks, active }, frames, minFrames, waitMs, hasBeats, sample: number[] }`.
  - `Segment` always has `{ kind: "hit" | "beat" | "line", start, frames, hp, marks, active }`.
    - A `hit` adds `event`, whose notes are rebuilt.
    - A `beat` adds `fx: "brn" | "psn" | "tox" | "par" | "slp" | "frz" | "confusion" | "leechseed" | "stats" | "cure"`, `side`, `slot`, `text`, `hpChanges: [side, slot, from, to][]`, `marksAfter`, `ko: boolean`, and, for `stats` only, `up`, `down` and `pop` (such as `"ATK ▲▲"`).
    - A `line` adds `text`.
  - `hp` and `marks` are the values at the segment's start.

  - **End of turn.** The burn, poison, and bad poison ticks each give a beat whose `hpChanges` from and to match the step's HP before and after. Leech Seed gives one `leechseed` beat with two `hpChanges` (the target down, the seeder up), and the seeder's heal line isn't in any text.

  - **Hitless turns.** Both sides asleep: `segments` are all beats or lines, and the next turn's first hit doesn't repeat them.
  - **Invariants, over 40 seeded fights with both sides on `plan: "status"` and on `plan: "setup"`, for every turn:**
    - `end.hp` deep-equals `snapshots[turn]`.
    - `end.marks.status` deep-equals `conditions[turn].status`.

    - `structuredClone(t)` deep-equals `t`. (Review Focus 5)

    - No beats: `waitMs === 2000` and `minFrames === max(hits × 7 + 10, 40)`.
    - With beats: `waitMs === max(2000, frames × 100 + 600)` and `minFrames === max(frames + 10, ceil((waitMs + 2000) / 100))`.
  - **`sample`** holds `start + floor(frames / 2)` of the first beat of each `fx`.
  - **`BATTLE_FX=0`:** `turnTimeline(r, t, names, { env: { BATTLE_FX: "0" } })` deep-equals `hitsTimeline(log[t - 1], snapshots[t - 1], activeAt(r, t))`, and its events are `log[t - 1]`'s own objects with their notes unchanged.

- [ ] **Step 3: Implement `turnTimeline`.** It walks `steps[turn − 1]` once, with running `hp` (from `snapshots[turn − 1]`), `marks` (from `marksAt(result, turn − 1)`, or empty), and `active` (`activeAt` before turn 1's switches, from `playback.js`; move `activeAt` into `timeline.js` and re-export it from `playback.js`).     - `from: "brn"` gives a `brn` beat.
    - `from: "psn"` gives a `psn` beat, or a `tox` beat when the mark is `tox`.
    - `from: "Leech Seed"` gives a `leechseed` beat, and the next `hp` heal on the other side joins it.

  - **`switch`:** sets `active[side]` and clears `chips[side]`.

  - **Every other step with text:** stays on its hit, as `buildResult` places it. On a turn with no hit, it becomes a `line`.
  - **Rebuilt notes:** on hits, `preNotes` (before the first hit) and `notes` (after) are rebuilt from the remaining non-beat steps by `buildResult`'s rules, skipping a faint the hit already tells. Lines carried in from an earlier turn are dropped. `endNotes` is not used.
